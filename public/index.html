The problem. Rising LPG prices and empty tanks are an everyday frustration in Filipino homes.

The idea. Open on the stove clicking with no flame, then follow Jen from that moment to a parcel at the gate and her first induction-cooked meal, ending on a hero shot. Sound-off viewers get the problem from one super in the first 2.5 seconds.

The craft. Every shot was generated one at a time against fixed character, product and floorplan references, so Jen, the cooker and the kitchen stay consistent from the first frame to the last.

Brief (self-set)
A 60-second product spot that turns a familiar Filipino kitchen problem, the LPG tank running out, into a reason to switch to induction.
